Ask a question
Which IT courses are available at Zenmith Radiance?

We offer career-focused tracks in Java Full Stack Development, Python Full Stack Development, Web Development, Software Testing, Automation Testing, Data Analytics, DevOps & Cloud, and Agile & Scrum.

Can freshers or non-IT graduates join?

Yes. Training starts from strong fundamentals, so you can begin your IT journey or move into a new technology area without prior industry experience. The right starting point is discussed during counselling.

Is the training practical or theory-based?

The learning is practical and job-oriented. Concepts are reinforced through hands-on exercises, guided builds and project work that help you demonstrate what you can do.

Do the courses include placement support?

Yes. Career support includes resume and LinkedIn guidance, technical interview preparation, mock interviews, aptitude and communication practice, career counselling and placement opportunity updates.

Are classes available online or in Chennai?

Zenmith Radiance is based in Kolathur, Chennai. Classroom and instructor-led online options can be discussed during your enquiry so you can choose the format that fits your schedule.

How do I choose the right learning path?

Share your qualification, current experience and career goal with us. We will help you compare the tracks and identify a practical starting point based on where you want to go.

How can I enquire or join a batch?

Call or WhatsApp +91 96001 69790, email raviprasanth.zenmithradiance@gmail.com, or use the enquiry form on this page. We will share current course and batch details.
